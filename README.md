# 🛒 Super Market Sales & Forecast Analysis Dashboard (Power BI)

## 📌 Project Overview

This project showcases an **interactive Power BI dashboard** built to analyze **supermarket sales performance, profitability, customer behavior, regional trends, payment preferences, shipping efficiency, and short-term sales forecasting**. The dashboard is designed for **executive and business users** to support data-driven decision-making.

---

## 🎯 Business Objective

Retail businesses require clear visibility into:

* Sales and profit performance
* High-performing product categories
* Customer segment contribution
* Regional demand patterns
* Short-term demand forecasting

This dashboard consolidates these insights into a **single, easy-to-navigate reporting solution**.

---

## 📊 Key Features

* KPI cards for **Total Sales, Profit, Orders, and Avg Shipping Days**
* Category & sub-category performance analysis
* Customer segment and regional contribution insights
* Payment mode and shipping mode analysis
* Monthly trends with **15-day sales forecasting**
* Clean, executive-friendly layout with interactive slicers

---

## 🧠 Key Insights

* **Technology** is the top-performing category, with Phones driving the highest sales.
* The **Consumer segment** contributes the largest share of total revenue.
* **East and West regions** lead overall sales, with California as a key state.
* Customers strongly prefer **digital payment modes (Cards & Online)**.
* Short-term sales forecast indicates **stable and predictable demand**.

---

## 🛠 Tools & Technologies

* **Power BI Desktop** – Dashboard development
* **DAX** – Measures, KPIs, and time intelligence
* **Power Query** – Data transformation and cleaning
* **Data Modeling** – Relationships and analytics-ready structure
* **Time Series Forecasting** – Short-term demand analysis
